# Yuan-Kai Liu

*   *   *

**Postdoctoral Researcher | Geophysics, Stanford University**

I am a geophysicist specializing in the integration of **space geodesy (InSAR/GNSS)** and **statistical seismology** to characterize solid Earth deformation. My research focuses on quantifying geophysical processes across various spatial and temporal scales, including seismic cycles, aseismic transients, and tectonic plate motions.

Currently, I am working at **Stanford** to advance on open access remote sensing displacement products for volcano monitoring globally. Also to explore capability of InSAR in our understanding of surface and subsurface hydrological processes, like induced deformation and soil moisture. 

Previously, I earned my Ph.D. at the [Caltech Seismolab](https://www.seismolab.caltech.edu) under the mentorship of Profs. of [Mark Simons](http://web.gps.caltech.edu/~simons/) and [Zach Ross](http://web.gps.caltech.edu/~zross/). My prior work also includes investigating crustal deformation associated with caldera collapses during my master with Prof. [Sigurjón Jónsson](https://cdi.kaust.edu.sa/people/detail/sigurj%C3%B3n-j%C3%B3nsson) at KAUST (Saudi Arabia).

**Research Interests:** tectonic geodesy, InSAR, seismic cycles, plate tectonics, statistical seismology.
